01 — Concept
How the Game Works

The Higher Lower Game is a knowledge-based strategy game about guessing whether one item has a higher or lower monthly Google Search volume than another. You start with two items. Guess correctly → gain a point and advance. Guess wrong → round ends and your score resets. The item with the higher search volume stays as the benchmark, while the lower item is replaced.

02 — Engineering
How I Coded It

The core logic revolves around if-else statements. The search volumes for multiple items are stored in an array. Two are randomly selected at game start. When the user guesses correctly, the item with the lhigher search volume would slide left and the one with a lower volume would be replaced with a new item.
